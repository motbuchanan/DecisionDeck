    HOW TO BONK

    1. Pick a launcher up top — On Foot, Cannon, or Rocket.
    2. Set the DISTANCE — how far back he starts from the wall.
    3. Aim the ANGLE (Cannon & Rocket). The dashed line shows exactly where the shot lands before you fire.
    4. Set the POWER — running speed, cannon muzzle, or hold BURN for the rocket. While burning, slide your thumb up or down to steer.
    5. Hit GO. He picks himself up and walks back. LOOP repeats it forever.
    6. Grab him with a finger and fling him any time, in any mode.
    7. LIMP → STIFF sets how floppy he is on impact.
    8. Hit the target painted on the wall for points. It moves after every hit.
    9. awards holds your records and 20 achievements. blood and skin are toggles.
